  • The ronin: ronin.glb replaces the procedural cowboy via a puppet-rig facade — the GLB skeleton driven by the game's own procedural pose system (walk gait with foot IK, saddle-aware mount choreography, weapon IK), world-axis semantics, measured limb lengths fed back into PROP.
